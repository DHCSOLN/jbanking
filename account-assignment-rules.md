RULE 1 — Account numbers are preexisting bulk account numbers.
RULE 2 — Software SHALL NOT generate bank account numbers.
RULE 3 — One routing+account combination SHALL NOT belong to multiple wallets.
RULE 4 — An assigned account SHALL remain assigned to its wallet.
RULE 5 — Reopening a wallet SHALL return its existing assignment.
RULE 6 — Assignment SHALL occur under an atomic lock.
RULE 7 — Account and routing numbers SHALL be handled as strings.
RULE 8 — Vendor destination accounts SHALL NOT be treated as wallet accounts.
RULE 9 — Historical payment records SHALL NOT be account-allocation pools.
RULE 10 — Every assignment SHALL create an audit record.
